(01) — The founder

Sanchit
Chopra

Founder & CEO of Shutr — the sole founder, the first hire, and still the loudest voice in the edit suite.

Sanchit Chopra built Shutr around a simple, slightly unreasonable idea: brands don’t need more content — they need better frames. Ideas sharp enough to stop a thumb mid-scroll, and systems smart enough to keep the conversation going once it stops.

Sanchit works where taste meets technology: one hand on the moodboard, the other in the code. That’s why Shutr does two things most agencies keep apart — creative direction for social, and AI agents for brands. To Sanchit, they’re the same job: making a brand feel alive, everywhere, all the time.

“I started Shutr because I was tired of beautiful brands with boring feeds — and smart tech that sounded like nobody. We fix both.”
— Sanchit Chopra

(03) — House rules

Five rules. No exceptions.

  1. No boring posts.

    If it wouldn’t stop us scrolling, it doesn’t ship.

  2. Taste is a strategy.

    Good taste isn’t decoration. It’s the reason people choose you over the brand next door.

  3. Every frame earns its place.

    Fewer, better things. Then more of the better things.

  4. Ship, then sharpen.

    Momentum beats perfection. Real data tells us where to aim next.

  5. Humans first, bots second.

    Agents take the repetitive work so people can do the remarkable work.
