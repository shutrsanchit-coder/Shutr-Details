01The brief

The brief

Nova’s inbox was drowning in the same dozen questions — range, charging, delivery dates, test rides. Customers waited hours for answers a good salesperson could give in seconds.

02The background

The background

E-bikes are a considered purchase. People want reassurance before they buy and fast help after.

The team didn’t want a robotic FAQ bot. They wanted something that sounded like their best in-store rider: friendly, specific and never pushy.

03The idea

The idea

We built Nova — a concierge agent with the brand’s voice, the full product catalogue and the booking system behind it. It answers, recommends, books test rides and hands over to a human the moment a conversation needs one.

We wrote its personality like we’d write a brand: tone, boundaries, jokes it’s allowed to make, and the ones it isn’t.

“A chatbot answers questions. An agent gets things done.”
